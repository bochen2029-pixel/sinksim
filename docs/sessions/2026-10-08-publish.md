## Open questions and loose ends

- `docs/sessions/2026-10-08-phase2-cuda.md` and `docs/DETERMINISM.md` still describe the tree as local only; that

- The oracle could now be consumed as a pinned copy or submodule of the public repository at its commit 9379be7

  trusting an exact golden comparison on a new machine (see `third_party/README.md`).
